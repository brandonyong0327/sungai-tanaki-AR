How it works

1
Allow camera

Tap "Start scanning" and allow camera access. Everything runs in your browser.

2
Point at a page

Lay the book flat under bright light, hold the phone 25–40 cm away.

3
Watch it come alive

A 3D scene appears on the page and the story is read aloud.

Every page, a small world

Tap any scene to explore it in 3D, even without the book.

Works on Chrome (Android) and Safari (iPhone). Camera images are processed on your device only.
